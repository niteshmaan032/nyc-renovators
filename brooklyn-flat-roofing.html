What we install

Types of Flat Roofing We Install

Each system suits a different roof. We recommend one for the property, its drainage, and roof details.

TPO

Single-ply membrane

A white membrane with heat-welded seams. It reflects sunlight and keeps the top floor cooler.

Surface
White, reflective
Seams
Heat-welded
Best for
Mixed-use and commercial roofs

EPDM

Rubber membrane

A flexible rubber sheet laid in large pieces with few seams. It handles freeze and thaw well.

Surface
Black rubber
Seams
Taped or adhered
Best for
Brownstones and row houses

Modified Bitumen

SBS, multi-ply

Layered asphalt-based sheets that build a thick, tough roof that handles foot traffic well.

Surface
Granular cap sheet
Seams
Torched or self-adhered
Best for
Multifamily, bulkheads, parapets

Roof Coatings

Liquid-applied

A seamless reflective layer over a sound roof. It can delay a full replacement.

Surface
Seamless, reflective
Seams
None once cured
Best for
Roofs still in fair condition

Warning signs

When to Replace Your Flat Roof

Flat roofs rarely fail all at once. These are the signs that a repair may no longer be enough.

  • Leaks or ceiling stainsWater marks on the top floor after rain
  • Ponding waterPuddles still there days after a storm
  • Cracks and blistersSplits, bubbles, or open seams
  • Flashing damageLoose at parapets, vents, and skylights
  • Worn membraneExposed layers or a soft, spongy surface
  • Repeat repairsThe same area patched again and again

Flat Roof Replacement

  • Old roof removed down to the deck
  • Deck and drains checked before anything goes on
  • New system matched to the building

Good to know

Flat Roofing FAQs

Common questions Brooklyn property owners ask before a flat roof repair or replacement.

Get a Free Estimate All Brooklyn FAQs
Do you specialize in flat roofing in Brooklyn?

Yes. Flat roofing is one of our main services, for residential, multifamily, and commercial buildings throughout Brooklyn.

What flat roofing materials do you install?

Mostly EPDM, TPO, and modified bitumen, chosen to fit the building, how the roof is used, and your budget.

How do I know if my flat roof needs repair?

Watch for leaks, ponding water, cracks, worn membrane, damaged flashing, or water stains inside the property.

How long does a flat roof last?

It depends on the material and maintenance, but many systems last 20 years or longer with proper care.

Can you replace old flat roofs on Brooklyn brownstones?

Yes. We work on brownstones, townhouses, row homes, apartment buildings, and commercial properties.

Do you provide flat roof inspections and estimates?

Yes. We inspect the roof and give you a free estimate, so you understand its condition and the next steps.
