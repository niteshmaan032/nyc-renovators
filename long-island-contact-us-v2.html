FAQs

Estimate FAQs

Answers to frequently asked questions.

Call (516) 252-3001
How do I request a free estimate?

Call (516) 252-3001 and tell us about the property and the work you need. We answer 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Does an estimate cost anything?

No. We inspect the roof and give you a free estimate with recommendations based on your property’s needs.

Can you help after storm damage?

Yes. We repair storm-related roof damage. Prompt repairs help prevent further problems and restore protection, so call as soon as it is safe.

What information should I have ready?

Your address, the type of property, what is happening with the roof, and when we can come by to inspect it.

Which areas do you cover?

Nassau and Suffolk County, including Hempstead, Freeport, Garden City, Valley Stream, Great Neck, Huntington, Babylon, Islip, and surrounding communities. Our office is at 70 Sunrise Hwy, Suite 500, Valley Stream.

Royal Roofing & Siding Long Island provides roof repairs, replacements, siding, and gutter services throughout Nassau and Suffolk counties for 35+ years. From shingle roofs to flat roofing systems, we help homeowners and businesses protect their properties’ exteriors.
