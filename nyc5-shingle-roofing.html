FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

The things homeowners ask us most before a shingle roofing job. If yours is not here, ask and we will answer it just as plainly.

Ask us a question
How long do asphalt shingle roofs last in Queens?

Most asphalt shingle roofs last 20–30 years depending on material quality and maintenance. Regular inspections help extend lifespan.

How do I know if my shingle roof needs replacement?

Signs include curling shingles, missing pieces, frequent leaks and visible wear. Royal Renovators Inc. provides inspections to determine the best solution.

Are architectural shingles better than standard shingles?

Yes. Architectural shingles are thicker, more durable and often last longer while providing a more dimensional appearance.

Can damaged shingles be repaired instead of replaced?

Minor damage can often be repaired. If damage is widespread or the roof is aging, replacement may be recommended.

How long does shingle roof installation take?

Most residential installations are completed within a few days depending on roof size and weather conditions.

Do you offer warranties on shingle roofing work?

Yes. Royal Renovators Inc. provides warranty coverage on both materials and labor for long-term peace of mind.
