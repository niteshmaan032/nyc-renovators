FAQs

Before You Get in Touch

The things people ask most often before they call: how fast we reply, what an estimate costs and how far we travel. If yours is not here, put it in the form and we will answer it just as plainly.

Ask us a question
How quickly will I hear back?

Send the form and we come back to you within one business day. If the roof is actively leaking, call instead. The phone reaches someone seven days a week, including outside office hours.

Does an estimate cost anything?

No. We get up on the roof, photograph what we find and put a written, itemised price in front of you at no charge and with no obligation, including when the answer is that it does not need replacing yet.

Can you come out for an emergency?

Yes. Water does not wait for Monday. We take emergency calls every day of the week, make the building safe first and quote the permanent repair afterwards.

What should I tell you when I get in touch?

The borough, roughly what the roof is doing and how long it has been doing it. A photograph helps more than anything else, as it often tells us whether this is a repair or a replacement before we arrive.

Which areas do you cover?

All five boroughs, Long Island and lower Westchester, from our office in Forest Hills. If you are not sure whether we reach you, ask and we will tell you straight.
