Warning signs

When to Repair or Replace Your Flat Roof

Coastal winds, heavy rain, snow, humidity, and changing temperatures put constant stress on flat roofs. These problems start small, so catch them early and a repair is often enough.

  • Ponding waterPuddles that sit for days after rain
  • Seam separationLifting laps and open seams in the membrane
  • Membrane crackingCracks, blisters, and surface wear with age
  • Leaks and stainsWater stains on ceilings below the roof
  • Flashing damageGaps at walls, curbs, skylights, and edges
  • Aging patchesA roof covered in old repairs

Repair or Replace?

Repair often fits when

  • Damage is in one area
  • The membrane is serviceable
  • The deck is dry and solid

Replace when

  • Wear is across the roof
  • Leaks keep coming back
  • Water has soaked the deck

Flat Roofing FAQs

Do you provide flat roofing services throughout Long Island?

Yes. We provide flat roof installation, repair, replacement, and inspections throughout Long Island.

What flat roofing materials do you use?

We commonly install EPDM, TPO, and modified bitumen systems, depending on the building and the project’s needs.

How do I know if my flat roof needs repair?

Signs include leaks, ponding water, cracks, damaged seams, flashing problems, and water stains inside the property.

How long does a flat roof last?

It depends on the material and the maintenance, but many flat roofs last 20 years or more.

Can you replace old flat roofs on apartment buildings?

Yes. We work on residential, commercial, and multifamily properties throughout Long Island.
