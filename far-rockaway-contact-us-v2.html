Estimate FAQs

Answers to frequently asked questions.

How do I request a free estimate?

Call (718) 831-6489 and tell us about the property and the work you need. Or fill out the form, and we’ll call you back within the hour.

Can I call about an active roof leak or storm damage?

Yes. Call us so we can discuss the leak and the next step. We answer the phone 24 hours a day, seven days a week.

What information should I have ready?

Your property address, the type of roof or exterior, and a short description of the problem help us understand your project.

Which kinds of buildings do you work on?

We work on single-family and coastal homes, multifamily and mixed-use properties, and commercial buildings across Far Rockaway, Arverne, Edgemere, and nearby coastal communities.
