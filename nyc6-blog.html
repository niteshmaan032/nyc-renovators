How much does a roof replacement cost in Queens?

Replacement cost depends on the material, the size of the roof and its structural condition. An average-sized home’s shingle or flat roof usually costs about $10,000 to $15,000 to replace. A detailed estimate is provided after evaluation.

How long does a roof last in NYC?

Most roofs last 20 to 30 years depending on material quality and maintenance. Shingle roofs we replace come with a 50-year GAF factory-backed warranty. Flat roofs are expected to last 20 years once replaced and come with a 15-year Royal Renovators warranty.

Do I need a permit to replace my roof?

Sometimes. It depends on the borough, the building and the scope of work. We check before quoting and discuss it with you, and we work with architects and DOB expeditors when a permit is required.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most residential roofs are a one to two day job once we start. Flat roofs on large commercial buildings run longer, and weather can move a start date, but we tell you before we book, not after.

Does an estimate cost anything?

No. We get up on the roof, photograph what we find and put a written, itemized price in front of you at no charge and with no obligation.
