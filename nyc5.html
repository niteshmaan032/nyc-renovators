Roofing systems

Flat and Shingle Roofing for Every NYC Building Type

New York buildings require different roofing systems. Brownstones, row houses, apartment buildings, storefronts, and many extensions typically use low-slope roofing, while pitched homes generally use shingles. We install both, so we recommend the system that best fits the building.

  • Low-slope

    Flat Roofs

    Flat and low-slope roofs are common throughout Queens, Brooklyn, the Bronx, and Manhattan. Proper drainage, seams, flashing, and parapet details are critical to preventing leaks and standing water.

    • EPDM, TPO & Modified Bitumen
    • Drainage and Ponding Corrections
    • Parapet, Flashing & Penetration Detailing
    • Tapered Insulation Where Needed

    Typical service life15–25 years

  • Pitched

    Shingle Roofs

    For pitched roofs, we install complete GAF shingle systems on detached homes, two-family properties, dormers, extensions, and other sloped roof areas.

    • GAF Timberline Architectural Shingles
    • Roof Deck Inspected Before Installation
    • Ice & Water Shield at Eaves and Valleys
    • Proper Flashing, Ventilation & Ridge Caps

    Typical service life25–50 years

Flat and pitched on the same building?

Some NYC properties have both flat and pitched roof sections. We install and properly transition both systems when required.

Building types

Roofing Types and Building Systems in NYC

  • Flat · EPDM, TPO or modified bitumen

    Row Houses & Brownstones

    Shared parapets and a neighbor on either side are where these roofs leak. We detail the parapet and party wall properly.

  • Shingle · GAF architectural

    Two-Family & Semi-Detached Homes

    Pitched roofs with dormers, valleys and often a flat rear extension. Both systems in one job, with the transition flashed as a detail.

  • Flat · TPO with tapered insulation

    Storefronts & Mixed-Use

    A leak here reaches stock and tenants. We stage the work around trading hours and keep the building watertight at the end of every day.

  • Flat · Modified bitumen or single-ply

    Apartment Buildings

    Bulkheads, water tanks, HVAC curbs and vent stacks all have to be flashed. Scheduled around occupancy, with notice to residents.

  • Shingle · GAF Timberline system

    Detached Single-Family

    Full tear-off, deck inspected and repaired where it is soft, then the complete system installed to spec so the warranty registers.

  • Flat · Single-ply, large spans

    Warehouses & Industrial

    Large open areas where drainage and seam quality decide roof life. Heat-welded seams, fastened where the deck allows, maintenance plans available.

FAQs

Questions We Get Asked Most

Straight answers to the questions homeowners ask before they let anyone on the roof: what it costs, who files the permits, and what the warranty actually covers. If yours is not here, ask us and we will answer it.

Ask us a question
How long does a roof replacement take?

Most residential roofs are a one to two day job once we start. Flat roofs on large commercial buildings run longer, and weather can move a start date, but we tell you before we book, not after.

Do I need a permit to replace my roof?

Sometimes. It depends on the borough, the building and the scope of work. We check before quoting and discuss it with you.

What warranty do I get?

As GAF Master Elite roofing contractors, on shingle roofs we can offer a factory-backed labor and material warranty. Our own workmanship warranty sits on top of that and is written into the contract.

Can you work through the winter?

Yes, with limits. Some materials will not seal properly below a certain temperature, so we schedule around the forecast rather than the calendar and say plainly when a job should wait.

Do you help with insurance claims?

We document the damage with photographs and provide an itemized estimate your adjuster can work from. We are not public adjusters, so we do not negotiate the claim on your behalf.
