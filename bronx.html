Choose the right system

Flat vs. Shingle Roofing for Bronx Buildings

Most Bronx buildings have flat roofs, while many detached homes have pitched shingle roofs. Here is how the two systems compare.

Flat roofing compared with shingle roofing
Compare
Low-slopeFlat Roofing
PitchedShingle Roofing
Typical buildingsPrewar apartments, row houses, mixed-use buildings, and warehousesDetached and semi-detached homes, dormers, and pitched additions
Where in the BronxFordham, Pelham Bay, Morris Park, Hunts Point, and Port MorrisRiverdale and Country Club, and detached homes across the borough
MaterialsEPDM, TPO, and modified bitumen membranesArchitectural asphalt shingles over new underlayment
Watch forPonding water, membrane damage, flashing leaks, and age-related wearLifted or missing shingles, poor ventilation, and flashing at walls
What we doInspection, leak detection, repair, replacement, and new installationInstallation, replacement, repair, and help choosing styles and colors

Roofing for Every Bronx Building

From prewar walk-ups to detached homes, every Bronx building type has its own weak points. We match the roof system to the structure.

  • Flat · Modified bitumen or EPDM

    Prewar Apartments

    Bulkheads, water tanks, and parapets all need tight flashing. We schedule the work around tenants.

    Concourse, Kingsbridge, and Fordham

  • Flat · EPDM or TPO

    Row Houses

    Shared parapets and roofs that run into the next house are where leaks start. We seal the whole row.

    Fordham, Pelham Bay, Riverdale, Throgs Neck, and Morris Park

  • Shingle · Architectural asphalt

    Detached Homes

    Pitched roofs often meet a flat rear extension. We flash every transition and vent the attic properly.

    Riverdale and Country Club

  • Flat · TPO or modified bitumen

    Mixed-Use & Storefronts

    Apartments over a shop need work staged around business hours, with rooftop units kept sealed.

    Commercial streets across the Bronx

Frequently Asked Questions

See All FAQs Call 718-395-5061
Do you provide roofing services throughout the Bronx?

Yes. Royal Roofing Bronx provides roofing services across all Bronx neighborhoods, from Riverdale and Fordham to Throgs Neck and Hunts Point.

What roofing systems are common in the Bronx?

Flat roofing systems are common on many Bronx buildings, from row houses to apartment buildings. We specialize in installing and repairing durable flat roofs, and we install shingle roofs on pitched homes.

How do I know if my Bronx property needs roof repair?

Signs include leaks, water stains, missing materials, and visible damage. We provide professional inspections to find the cause before recommending a repair.

Do you handle commercial roofing in the Bronx?

Yes. We work with commercial property owners and managers across the Bronx, including warehouses, retail, and mixed-use buildings.

Do you offer exterior services besides roofing?

Yes. We provide siding, gutter, window, masonry, and other exterior services in addition to roofing.

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes. Royal Roofing Bronx offers free roofing estimates for residential and commercial property owners. Call 718-395-5061 to schedule yours.
