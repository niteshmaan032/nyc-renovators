Our process

How We Run Every Yonkers Project

No two projects are approached the same way, but the same four steps keep every roof on track.

  1. Free inspection

    We check the shingles or membrane, flashing, ventilation, decking, and drainage before recommending anything.

  2. Honest options

    If a repair is enough, we tell you. If replacement is the better investment, we explain why.

  3. Careful installation

    Quality materials and proven installation methods, with your property protected throughout.

  4. Final walkthrough

    We inspect the finished roof with you and leave the site clean.
