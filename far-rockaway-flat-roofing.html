Types of Flat Roofing We Install

We recommend EPDM, TPO, or modified bitumen depending on the structure, how the building is used, and your budget.

Warning signs

When Your Flat Roof Needs Attention

Many flat roofs start to fail when water stops draining. Some only need targeted repairs; others need a complete replacement. We tell you which, and why.

  • Ponding waterStanding water that weakens the membrane
  • Membrane separationEdges lifting or splitting after storms
  • Seam damageOpen seams that let moisture in
  • Flashing leaksAt parapets, drains, vents, and penetrations
  • Interior stainsCeiling stains and recurring moisture inside
  • Repeat repairsThe same area patched again and again

Repair & maintenance

Flat Roof Repair & Maintenance

Leaks, seam damage, and flashing problems get expensive when they are ignored. We find the source and fix it properly.

We repair:

  • Roof leaks and hidden moisture
  • Cracks, blisters, and open seams
  • Flashing and parapet walls
  • Ponding water and clogged drains
  • Storm and wind damage
  • Worn or damaged membrane

Routine maintenance checks drains, seams, flashing, and the roof surface at least once a year and after major storms, to catch problems early and extend the life of the roof.

Flat Roofing FAQs

Common questions Far Rockaway property owners ask before a flat roof repair or replacement.

Get a Free Estimate All Far Rockaway FAQs
Do you provide flat roofing services throughout Far Rockaway?

Yes. We provide flat roof installation, repair, replacement, and inspections throughout Far Rockaway and the nearby coastal communities.

What flat roofing materials do you install?

We commonly install EPDM, TPO, and modified bitumen systems, based on the building’s needs and your budget.

Why do flat roofs wear faster near the coast?

Salt air, strong winds, moisture, and storms speed up wear when the roof is not maintained properly.

How do I know if my flat roof needs repair?

Look for ponding water, seam damage, flashing leaks, interior stains, and visible cracks.

Can you replace old flat roofs on mixed-use buildings?

Yes. We work on homes, multifamily buildings, mixed-use properties, and commercial roofs in Far Rockaway.

Do you offer inspections and estimates?

Yes. We inspect the roof and give you a free estimate, so you understand its condition and the next steps.
