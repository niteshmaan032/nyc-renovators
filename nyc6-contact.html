FAQs

Contact & Estimate FAQs

Answers about response times, free estimates, emergency service, and the areas we cover.

Request an Estimate
How quickly will I hear back?

Send the form and we get back to you within one day. If the roof is actively leaking, call instead. The phone reaches someone seven days a week, including outside office hours.

Does an estimate cost anything?

No. We get up on the roof, photograph what we find and put a written, itemized price in front of you at no charge and with no obligation, including when the answer is that it does not need replacing yet.

Can you come out for an emergency?

Yes. Water does not wait for Monday. We take emergency calls every day of the week, make the building safe first and quote the permanent repair afterwards.

What should I tell you when I get in touch?

The borough, roughly what the roof is doing and how long it has been doing it.

Which areas do you cover?

All five boroughs of NYC, Long Island, Yonkers, and Westchester.

Royal Renovators Inc. is a licensed NYC roofing contractor serving Queens, Brooklyn, Manhattan, the Bronx, Yonkers, and Long Island since 1988. We specialize in flat roofing, shingles, roof repair, siding, gutters, and masonry.
