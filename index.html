Factory certified

GAF Master Elite Roofing Trusted Throughout New York City

GAF is the largest roofing manufacturer in North America, and fewer than 2% of roofing contractors carry its Master Elite certification. Royal Renovators Inc. is one of them — and has been installing GAF systems on New York roofs for decades.

Master Elite is not a badge a contractor can buy. GAF only awards it to companies that are properly licensed, carry full insurance, and hold a documented record of work that stands up over time. Certification is reviewed, not granted once and forgotten, so it stays a live measure of how a company actually works.

For you, the practical difference is the warranty. Certified contractors can register warranties that the manufacturer stands behind directly — covering the workmanship as well as the materials — instead of a promise that only lasts as long as the contractor does.

Roofing systems

Flat and Shingle Roofing for Every NYC Building Type

New York does not have one kind of roof. A Brooklyn brownstone, a Forest Hills two-family, a Queens Boulevard storefront and a Bronx apartment building each fail in their own way and each need a different system. We install both flat and shingle roofs, so the recommendation you get is based on the building — not on the one product a company happens to sell.

Two systems, chosen by the roof

  • Low-slope

    Flat roofs

    Flat roofs cover most of the low-slope building stock in Queens, Brooklyn and the Bronx. We have been working on them since 1988, so we know how they fail: water ponding at the low point, seams splitting as the temperature swings, and flashing that was never detailed properly where the roof meets the parapet.

    A flat roof is a drainage problem before it is a covering problem. We survey where the water actually sits, correct the falls with tapered insulation where the deck will not drain on its own, and detail every parapet, party wall and penetration before the membrane goes down.

    • EPDM, TPO and modified bitumen systems
    • Heat-welded or fully adhered seams
    • Tapered insulation to correct standing water
    • Parapet, party-wall and coping detailing
    • Drains, scuppers and leaders cleared and reset

    Typical service life15–25 years

  • Pitched

    Shingle roofs

    Shingle roofs go on the pitched stock — detached and semi-detached homes, dormers, extensions and porches. As a GAF Master Elite contractor we install architectural shingles as a complete system rather than as a covering laid over whatever is already up there.

    That means the deck gets inspected and repaired where it is soft, ice and water shield goes down at the eaves and in the valleys, and the ridge is vented. Skip any one of those layers and the warranty behind the roof stops meaning very much.

    • GAF Timberline architectural shingles
    • Deck inspected and repaired before covering
    • Ice and water shield at eaves and valleys
    • Starter course, field shingles, ridge vent and cap
    • Step and counter flashing at walls and chimneys

    Typical service life25–40 years

On either system we quote tear-off and overlay separately, so you can see what each one actually costs, and we say plainly when a roof still has years left in it. Work is scheduled around the forecast rather than the calendar, so nothing gets opened up the day before rain.

FAQs

Questions We Get Asked Most

Straight answers to the things homeowners ask before they let anyone on the roof — what it costs, who files the permits, what the warranty actually covers and how long you will have people on site. If yours is not here, ask us and we will answer it just as plainly.

Ask us a question
What does a new roof cost in New York?

Square footage is only the starting point. Access, disposal, deck repairs and permit fees are where quotes really diverge, which is why we itemise all of it rather than giving a price per square.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most residential roofs are a two to four day job once we start. Flat roofs on larger buildings run longer, and weather can move a start date — we tell you before we book, not after.

Do I need a permit to replace my roof?

Sometimes. It depends on the borough, the building and the scope of work. We check before quoting and we file for it ourselves when one is needed.

What warranty do I get?

Manufacturer warranties come from the material — as a GAF Master Elite contractor we can offer cover that most roofers cannot. Our own workmanship warranty sits on top of that and is written into the contract.

Can you work through the winter?

Yes, with limits. Some materials will not seal properly below a certain temperature, so we schedule around the forecast rather than the calendar and say plainly when a job should wait.

Do you help with insurance claims?

We document the damage with photographs and provide an itemised estimate your adjuster can work from. We are not public adjusters, so we do not negotiate the claim on your behalf.
