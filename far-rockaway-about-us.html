Built for the coast

Built for Coastal Conditions & Far Rockaway Properties

Royal Roofing & Siding Far Rockaway was set up to meet the needs of properties near the coast, where weather works hard on roofs, drainage, and structural components.

What coastal roofs face

  • Salt airCorrosion and faster material wear.
  • MoistureHumidity and driving rain at seams.
  • Ocean windsLifted shingles and loose flashing.
  • Seasonal stormsStorm damage and freeze cycles.

Buildings we work on

  • Beachfront homesThe roofs most exposed to the ocean.
  • Single-family homesShingle and flat roofs, flashing, vents.
  • Residential buildingsFlat roofs planned around tenants.
  • Mixed-use structuresFlat roofs over storefronts.
