Warning signs

When to Repair or Replace Your Flat Roof

Heavy snow, freeze-thaw cycles, spring rain, and tree debris wear a flat roof down. Catch these early and a repair is often enough.

  • Ponding waterStanding water that stays long after rain
  • Clogged drainsLeaves and debris blocking drains and gutters
  • Membrane wearA worn, split, or lifting membrane surface
  • Leaks or ceiling stainsWater marks on top-floor ceilings after rain
  • Loose flashingGaps at parapets, skylights, and vents
  • Snow and ice at drainsIce forming around drainage areas in winter

Repair or Replace?

A repair often makes sense when

  • The damage is in one area
  • The rest of the membrane is sound
  • The deck underneath is dry and solid

A replacement is usually better when

  • The membrane is worn across the roof
  • Leaks keep coming back after repairs
  • The deck or insulation is wet

Flat Roofing FAQs

See All FAQs Get a Free Estimate
Is flat roofing a good option for homes in Yonkers?

Yes. Flat roofing works well for home additions, garages, modern homes, porches, and other low-slope residential structures.

Which flat roofing system performs best during Yonkers winters?

It depends on the building, but EPDM, TPO, and modified bitumen all perform well when installed professionally with proper insulation and drainage.

How does heavy snowfall affect flat roofs?

Snow adds load and can lead to ice around drainage areas. Routine inspections keep the roof performing safely through winter.

How often should commercial flat roofs be inspected?

At least once a year, and after major storms or heavy snowfall, so developing issues are found early.

Why is drainage so important on flat roofs?

Good drainage prevents standing water, slows membrane wear, protects the insulation, and extends the life of the whole roof.
