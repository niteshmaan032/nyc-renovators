A local company

Based in Forest Hills,
Working Across Queens

Our office is on Queens Boulevard in Forest Hills, and most of our crews live in the borough. When you call, you reach the people who will price the job, schedule it, and stand behind it afterward.

We have roofed homes and buildings in almost every Queens neighborhood, so we know the housing stock: brick row houses with shared parapets in Astoria and Ridgewood, Tudors with steep slopes in Forest Hills Gardens, two-family homes with a flat rear extension in Woodside, and prewar co-ops along the boulevard.

What we specialize in

  • Flat roof replacement in EPDM, TPO, and modified bitumen
  • GAF shingle roofs on single-family and two-family homes
  • Leak tracing and repairs at parapets, flashing, and drains
  • Siding, gutters, and masonry that keep water off the walls
