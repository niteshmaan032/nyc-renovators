Choose the right system

Flat & Shingle Roofing in Queens

The roof that works on a multifamily building is different from the roof on a pitched Queens home. We inspect the building, explain the options, and install the system that fits.

Many Queens properties carry both. A two-family house in Woodside or Middle Village often has a shingled front and a flat roof over the rear extension, and a mixed-use building on Steinway Street pairs a flat roof with a pitched bay over the store. We price both roofs in one estimate and flash the joint between them as a detail, since that seam is where water usually gets in.

Building types

Roofing Types and Building Systems in Queens

  • Shingle · GAF Timberline system

    Detached Single-Family Homes

    Common in Forest Hills, Bayside and across eastern Queens. Full tear-off, deck checked and repaired where it is soft, then the complete system installed so the warranty registers.

  • Flat · EPDM, TPO or modified bitumen

    Attached Row Houses

    The long blocks of Astoria, Jackson Heights and Ridgewood. Shared parapets and a neighbor on either side are where these roofs leak, so we detail the parapet and party wall properly.

  • Shingle + flat · Two systems, one job

    Two-Family & Semi-Detached Homes

    Pitched fronts with dormers and valleys, and often a flat rear extension. Both roofs in one job, with the transition between them flashed as a detail.

  • Steep shingle · Architectural or slate-look

    Tudor & Colonial Homes

    Steep, cut-up roofs in Forest Hills Gardens and Douglaston. Valleys, turrets and chimneys are flashed by hand, and the shingle is chosen to suit the architecture.

  • Flat · Modified bitumen or single-ply

    Garden Apartments & Prewar Co-ops

    Bulkheads, water tanks, HVAC curbs and vent stacks all have to be flashed. Scheduled with the board or managing agent, with notice to residents.

  • Flat · TPO with tapered insulation

    Mixed-Use Storefronts

    Shops with apartments above on the commercial strips. We stage the work around trading hours and keep the building watertight at the end of every day.

Commercial roofing

Commercial Roofing Services in Queens

Roughly a fifth of our work is on commercial roofs. We repair, replace, and maintain roofs for warehouses, stores, and restaurants across the borough, and plan every job around the business underneath.

  • Warehouses & industrial

    Large low-slope roofs in Long Island City, Maspeth, and College Point, with TPO or modified bitumen and drainage planned for wide spans.

  • Stores & mixed-use

    Work staged around opening hours, with the roof made watertight at the end of every day so stock and tenants stay dry.

  • Restaurants

    Exhaust fans, grease vents, and rooftop HVAC curbs flashed properly, since that is where restaurant roofs usually leak.

Inspections, repairs, maintenance plans, and full replacements, with a written scope before work starts.
