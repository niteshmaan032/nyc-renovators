Flat roof systems

Materials Built for Queens Roofs

We install high-quality roofing systems such as EPDM, TPO, SBS, and modified bitumen, all known for their durability and resistance to extreme weather conditions, with proper drainage design, membrane installation, and insulation.

EPDM RoofingA durable rubber membrane for flat and low-slope Queens roofs, with sealed seams and penetrations.
TPO RoofingA single-ply membrane that reflects heat and resists the extreme weather Queens buildings face.
SBS RoofingA rubber-modified (SBS) bitumen membrane that stays flexible through Queens freeze-thaw cycles.
Modified BitumenA layered asphalt-based system built for low-slope roofs with proper drainage design.

Building types

Roofing Types and Building Systems in Queens

  • Shingle · GAF Timberline system

    Detached Single-Family Homes

    Common in Forest Hills, Bayside and across eastern Queens. Full tear-off, deck checked and repaired where it is soft, then the complete system installed so the warranty registers.

  • Flat · EPDM, TPO or modified bitumen

    Attached Row Houses

    The long blocks of Astoria, Jackson Heights and Ridgewood. Shared parapets and a neighbor on either side are where these roofs leak, so we detail the parapet and party wall properly.

  • Shingle + flat · Two systems, one job

    Two-Family & Semi-Detached Homes

    Pitched fronts with dormers and valleys, and often a flat rear extension. Both roofs in one job, with the transition between them flashed as a detail.

  • Steep shingle · Architectural or slate-look

    Tudor & Colonial Homes

    Steep, cut-up roofs in Forest Hills Gardens and Douglaston. Valleys, turrets and chimneys are flashed by hand, and the shingle is chosen to suit the architecture.

  • Flat · Modified bitumen or single-ply

    Garden Apartments & Prewar Co-ops

    Bulkheads, water tanks, HVAC curbs and vent stacks all have to be flashed. Scheduled with the board or managing agent, with notice to residents.

  • Flat · TPO with tapered insulation

    Mixed-Use Storefronts

    Shops with apartments above on the commercial strips. We stage the work around trading hours and keep the building watertight at the end of every day.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is a trusted roofing contractor in Queens?

Royal Renovators Inc. is a roofing contractor with 35+ years of experience in roofing, siding, and gutter work across Queens.

What roofing services do you provide?

Roof repair, roof replacement, installation, inspection, and leak detection for flat roofing systems and shingle roofing for homes and commercial buildings.

Do you specialize in flat roofing?

Yes. We install and repair EPDM, TPO, SBS, and modified bitumen for lasting waterproof protection.

Does my roof need repair or replacement?

A minor leak, missing shingles, or damaged flashing may call for roof repairs. Extensive leaks or roof damage may call for a full replacement. We will inspect and advise.

Do you offer exterior services too?

Yes. Siding installation, gutter systems, and exterior repairs that protect the building envelope.

What areas of Queens do you serve?

All neighborhoods of Queens, including Astoria, Flushing, Jamaica, Forest Hills, Long Island City, Ridgewood, Bayside, Jackson Heights, and Rego Park.
