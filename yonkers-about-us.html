Before a recommendation

Look beyond the surface.

Heavy snowfall, freezing temperatures, spring rainstorms, strong winds, and humid summers put constant stress on a roof. Every project starts with a full inspection, so you decide on repair, replacement, or maintenance with the facts in front of you.

Roof surface

Shingles & membranes

Worn, lifted, or damaged roofing shows how the roof has handled the weather and what it needs next.

Roof edge

Flashing

Chimneys, skylights, vents, and roof transitions are where water gets in. We check each one before deciding what the work needs to cover.

Water path

Drainage & gutters

Clogged gutters and drains back water up under the roof. Knowing where water goes after a storm shapes the right scope.

Below the finish

Ventilation & decking

Trapped heat and moisture age a roof early. The deck and insulation tell us whether a repair or a replacement makes more sense.
