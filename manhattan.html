Choose the right system

Flat vs. Shingle Roofing in Manhattan

Most Manhattan buildings have flat roofs. Pitched shingle roofs appear on townhouse additions, dormers, and smaller homes.

Low-slope · Most Manhattan roofs

Flat Roofing

Waterproof membranes with tapered drainage and tight flashing at parapets, bulkheads, and rooftop equipment.

Typical buildings
Co-ops, condos, high-rises, and brownstones
Materials
TPO, EPDM, and modified bitumen (SBS) membranes
Watch for
Ponding water, membrane wear, and flashing leaks

Pitched · Townhouses and additions

Shingle Roofing

Asphalt and architectural shingles over new underlayment, with ventilation and sealing done right.

Typical buildings
Townhouse additions, dormers, and smaller pitched roofs
Materials
Asphalt and architectural shingles, new underlayment
Watch for
Lifted or missing shingles and poor ventilation

Who is a reliable roofing contractor in Manhattan?

Royal Roofing & Siding NYC serves Manhattan with more than 35 years of roofing and siding experience, from flat roofs on co-ops and townhouses to commercial and high-rise buildings.

What roofing services do you provide in Manhattan?

Roof installation, roof repair, roof replacement, flat roofing systems, shingle roofing, siding, gutters, and exterior repairs.

Do you specialize in flat roofing?

Yes. Flat roofing is one of our core specialties because most Manhattan buildings have flat roofs. We install and repair TPO, EPDM, and modified bitumen systems.

Do you work with co-op boards and building management?

Yes. We understand building access, safety requirements, and coordination with boards, managing agents, and supers, and we plan the work around residents and tenants.

How do I know if my roof needs repair?

Leaks, water stains on top-floor ceilings, damaged shingles, and ponding water on flat roofs are common signs. We inspect the roof to find the cause before recommending a repair.

Do you offer free estimates in Manhattan?

Yes. We provide free roofing estimates and consultations for property owners throughout Manhattan. Call 212-457-1331 to schedule yours.
