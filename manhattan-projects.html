Our process

How We Run Manhattan Projects

Dense blocks, shared walls, and boards to answer to. The same four steps keep every Manhattan job on track.

  1. Board approvals

    We send the scope, schedule, and insurance papers your board or managing agent asks for.

  2. Access & permits

    We plan roof access, hoisting, sidewalk protection, and any permits the job needs.

  3. Our own crew

    In-house crews install every system and leave the roof watertight at the end of each day.

  4. Final walkthrough

    We walk the finished roof with you or the super, test the drains, and clean up.
