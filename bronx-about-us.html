Who we are

Trusted Roofing Specialists Serving the Bronx

Royal Roofing Bronx provides reliable roofing, gutter, and masonry services for homeowners, landlords, and property managers throughout the Bronx.

The borough mixes detached homes, row houses, multifamily buildings, and mixed-use spaces. Each differs in design, age, and weather exposure, so we tailor every job to the building.

Bronx properties often mean flat roofs, older masonry, and multi-unit buildings. Owners rely on us for honest recommendations, quality workmanship, and dependable service.

Built for the Bronx

Built for Bronx Properties & Building Conditions

Royal Roofing Bronx was set up to serve Bronx properties. We know how the borough’s weather works on roofs, drainage systems, and structural components.

What Bronx roofs face

  • Heavy rainfallPonding water and overworked drains.
  • Winter snowSnow load and meltwater at parapets.
  • Temperature swingsSeams and flashing crack over time.
  • Long-term wearOld roofs and masonry let water in.

Buildings we work on

  • Detached homesShingle roofs, flashing, and vents.
  • Row housesFlat roofs with shared parapets.
  • Multifamily buildingsFlat roofs planned around tenants.
  • Mixed-use spacesFlat roofs over storefronts.
