FAQs

Before You Book

The questions homeowners ask most often once they have decided something needs doing but are not yet sure what.

Ask us a question
Which service do I actually need?

Usually a repair if the roof is otherwise sound, and a replacement once the deck or the number of past patches says otherwise. The free inspection is what settles it — we will tell you plainly if the roof has years left in it.

Do you handle everything, or just the roof?

Everything on the envelope. Roofing is the bulk of our work, but siding, gutters and masonry are often part of the same job — and a leak blamed on the roof turns out to be the parapet more often than you would think.

How quickly can you start?

Repairs are usually within a few days and emergencies the same day. Full replacements depend on permits, materials and the forecast — we give you a real date before you commit, not after.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Licensed NYC contractor #2030146, fully insured, BBB A+ accredited and GAF Factory-Certified Master Elite. We are happy to send the certificates before anyone sets foot on your property.
