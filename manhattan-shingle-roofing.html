Warning signs

When to Repair or Replace Your Shingle Roof

Wind between buildings, heavy rain, winter freeze-thaw, and summer heat all wear shingles down. Catch these signs early and a repair is often enough.

  • Curling or cracked shinglesEdges lifting, bowing, or splitting with age
  • Missing shinglesBare patches at ridges, corners, and edges
  • Granules in the guttersThe protective surface washing off the roof
  • Leaks after stormsCeiling stains or damp decking after rain
  • Dark streaks or saggingMoisture trapped in the roof or the deck
  • Loose flashingGaps at chimneys, walls, vents, and valleys

Repair or Replace?

Repair often fits when

  • Damage is in one area
  • The rest of the roof is sound
  • The decking is solid

Replace when

  • Wear is across the roof
  • Leaks keep coming back
  • The decking is water-damaged

Shingle Roofing FAQs

Do you provide shingle roofing services throughout Manhattan?

Yes. We install, repair, replace, and inspect shingle roofs throughout Manhattan, from Uptown brownstones to Downtown townhouses.

Are shingles suitable for Manhattan townhouses and brownstones?

Yes. Shingles are a practical and attractive option for pitched roof sections, including dormers, rear extensions, and rooftop additions.

How do I know if my shingle roof needs repair?

Look for missing shingles, curling edges, cracked shingles, leaks, and granules collecting in the gutters. If you see any of these, call us for an inspection.

Can wind damage only part of a roof?

Yes. Wind between buildings often hits ridges, corners, and exposed edges harder than the rest of the roof, so a localized repair may be all you need.

Can a new shingle roof improve property value?

Yes. A professionally installed roof improves curb appeal and long-term value, and it gives buyers confidence on premium residential buildings.

Do you offer inspections and estimates?

Yes. We provide professional inspections and free estimates based on the roof’s condition and your project. Call 212-457-1331 to schedule yours.
