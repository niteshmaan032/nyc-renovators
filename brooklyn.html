Flat roof systems

Materials Built for Brooklyn Flat Roofs

We install and maintain EPDM, TPO, modified bitumen, and SBS with the drainage and waterproofing that stop ponding water and membrane damage.

EPDM RoofingA flexible rubber membrane for flat and low-slope roofs, with seams and penetrations planned around the building.
TPO RoofingA light-colored single-ply membrane that reflects heat and suits roofs where layout and detailing make it the right fit.
Modified BitumenA layered asphalt-based system used on many low-slope roofs, with attention to transitions and drainage.
SBS & Parapet DetailsSBS membranes with sealed parapet walls and flashing, the details that keep brownstone roofs dry.
