Building types

Roofing Types and Building Systems in Brooklyn

Every Brooklyn building has its own weak points. We match the roof system to the structure, the drainage, and the way the building is used.

  • Flat · EPDM, TPO, or modified bitumen

    Brownstones & Row Houses

    Shared parapets, party walls, and a neighbor on either side are where these roofs leak. We seal the parapet, coping, and the seams between roofs.

  • Flat · Modified bitumen or SBS

    Prewar Walk-Ups & Multifamily

    Bulkheads, skylights, water tanks, and vent stacks all need proper flashing. We schedule the work around tenants, with notice to residents.

  • Flat · TPO with tapered insulation

    Mixed-Use & Storefront Buildings

    Apartments over a shop on the avenue. We work around business hours and leave the building watertight at the end of every day.

  • Shingle · Architectural asphalt

    Detached & Semi-Detached Homes

    Pitched roofs in Bay Ridge, Flatbush, and south Brooklyn, often with a flat rear extension. We flash the transition between both systems.

  • Flat or shingle · Roof and siding

    Frame Houses With Siding

    Common in Greenpoint, Bushwick, and Sunset Park. We plan the roof, gutters, and siding together so water leaves the building.

  • Flat · TPO or modified bitumen

    Condos, Co-ops & Lofts

    Larger roofs with decks, pavers, and rooftop units. We work with boards and managing agents and protect the finished space below.
