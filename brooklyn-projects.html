Our process

How We Run Every Project

The same four steps on every Brooklyn job, from a brownstone leak to a full commercial roof.

  1. 01

    Inspect the roof

    We check the roof surface, seams, flashing, drains, and parapets to find the real cause.

  2. 02

    Scope it in writing

    You get a clear scope and the roof system that fits the building, before any work starts.

  3. 03

    Our own crew installs

    In-house crews use premium materials and leave the roof watertight at the end of each day.

  4. 04

    Final walkthrough

    We walk the finished roof with you, test the drains, and clean up the site completely.
