More FAQs
Is the roofing estimate free?

Yes. Tell us about your property and the work you have in mind, and we can discuss the next step toward a free estimate.

What information should I have ready?

Your property address, a brief description of the issue, and when you first noticed it are a useful start. If you know your roof type or where a leak appears, share that too.

Do you work on both flat and shingle roofs?

Yes. We work on flat and shingle roofing, including repairs, replacements, inspections, and maintenance. We also handle siding, gutters, masonry, and other exterior work.

Do you serve areas beyond Queens?

Yes. Our office is in Forest Hills, and our roofing and exterior crews serve properties across New York City. Call with your address so we can discuss your project.

What should I do if my roof is leaking now?

Call us at (718) 414-6067 and describe what you are seeing. We can discuss the condition and the next useful step for your property.

When can I call your team?

Our listed phone hours are every day from 7:00 am to 11:00 pm. Call (718) 414-6067 to speak with our team.
