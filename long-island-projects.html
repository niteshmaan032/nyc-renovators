Our process

How We Run Every Project

Every project is tailored to the property’s design, budget, and long-term protection goals, from a single leak to a full new roof.

  1. 01

    Inspect the roof

    A full inspection to find hidden damage, drainage problems, and worn materials.

  2. 02

    Recommend the right fix

    Honest options for repair or replacement, based on the roof and your budget.

  3. 03

    Install with care

    Our crew uses quality materials and proven methods built for Long Island weather.

  4. 04

    Walk through the result

    We review the finished roof with you and clean up the site.
