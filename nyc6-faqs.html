Costs & Estimates

What roofing work costs in Queens and NYC, and how we price it.

How much does roof repair cost in Queens?

Roof repair costs vary with the severity of the damage. A small repair can cost as little as $1,000, including all labor, materials, insurance, vehicle expenses and project management. We provide a free written estimate after inspecting the roof.

How much does roof replacement cost in Queens?

Replacement cost depends on the material, the size of the roof and its structural condition. An average-sized residential home’s shingle roof or flat roof usually costs about $10,000 to $15,000 to replace. A detailed estimate is provided after evaluation.

How much does flat roof replacement cost in Queens?

A standard residential flat roof replacement costs about $10,000 to $15,000, depending on the material and size. Larger commercial roofs are priced after inspection.

What affects roofing costs in NYC?

Roof size, materials, accessibility, structural condition, building height and permit requirements all affect the total cost. Access and disposal are where quotes in the city differ most.

Is flat roofing cheaper than shingle roofing?

Flat roofing is the most cost-effective system for certain property types, especially commercial and multi-unit buildings. Per square foot, though, flat roofs generally cost more to replace than shingle roofs.

Do you provide written roofing estimates?

Yes. Every estimate visit ends with a transparent, written estimate that sets out the materials, the scope and the price. There is no charge and no obligation, including when the answer is that the roof does not need replacing yet.

Does roof replacement increase home value?

Yes. A new roof improves structural protection and energy efficiency, and it lifts resale value because the next owner does not have to replace it.

Does replacing a roof improve energy efficiency?

Yes. A new roofing system improves insulation and reduces heating and cooling costs.

Repair or Replace?

How to tell what your roof actually needs, and how long it should last.

How do I know if my roof needs repair?

Common signs are leaks, water stains, missing shingles, cracked membranes and ponding water. It is often better not to wait for a leak; by then the damage is already inside.

How do I know if I need a roof replacement?

If your roof is over 20 years old, leaking frequently or structurally compromised, replacement may be necessary. This is especially true in NYC, where multiple roofing layers installed over the years add dangerous weight to the rafters.

Is a roof repair better than a roof replacement?

Minor damage can often be repaired. Severe or widespread deterioration usually calls for full replacement. It is better to do one replacement and get a warranty than a string of repairs that end up costing more and often come with no warranty at all.

Can you repair small roof leaks?

Yes. Minor leaks found through leak detection can often be repaired quickly with a smaller crew, before they turn into structural damage.

How long does a roof last in NYC?

Most roofs last 20 to 30 years depending on material quality and maintenance. Shingle roofs we replace come with a 50-year GAF factory-backed warranty. Flat roofs are expected to last 20 years once replaced and come with a 15-year Royal Renovators warranty.

What shortens a roof’s lifespan?

Poor installation, lack of maintenance, clogged drainage and prolonged exposure to severe NYC weather. Proper installation and routine inspections significantly extend the life of a roof.

How often should roofs be inspected?

At least once a year and after major storms. Regular inspections catch small issues early, prevent costly structural damage and extend the roof’s lifespan. Our inspections are free.

Flat Roofing

The most common roof in the five boroughs, and our specialty.

Why is flat roofing common in NYC?

Many residential and commercial buildings in the city are built with low-slope roofs, and flat roofing materials are designed to handle their slow water drainage.

What flat roofing materials does Royal Renovators Inc. install?

EPDM, TPO, SBS and modified bitumen membranes, along with asphalt shingle systems for pitched roofs.

How long does a flat roof last?

A professionally installed flat roof lasts 20 to 30 years with proper maintenance, most of that time covered by the contractor’s warranty.

What causes flat roof leaks?

Ponding water, membrane punctures, flashing damage and poor drainage are the usual causes.

How often should flat roofs be inspected?

At least once a year and after major storms. The inspection is free, so it is better to be safe than sorry.

Are flat roofs safe during heavy rainfall?

Yes. When properly installed with the correct drainage, a flat roof handles very heavy rainfall without problems.

Is flat roofing a good investment in Queens?

Yes. Flat roofing suits the low-slope buildings across Queens and NYC, and a single, warrantied replacement costs less over time than repeated repairs.

Residential & Commercial

From two-family homes and brownstones to warehouses and mixed-use buildings.

Do you replace roofs on brownstones in Queens?

Yes. We specialize in roofing for brownstones and townhouses in Queens and throughout NYC, including Brooklyn’s historic homes and Manhattan brownstones.

What roofing materials are best for NYC homes?

Flat roofing systems and asphalt shingles are the most common choices, and the roof’s shape decides. Low-slope roofs need flat-roof materials that handle ponding water; a sharply pitched roof is best served by shingles.

How long does a residential roof replacement take?

Most projects finish within one to two days depending on size and complexity. Many of our replacements are done in a single day thanks to the manpower and systems we have built, which is unusual in the industry.

Do you install new roofs on newly constructed homes?

Yes. We provide roof installation for new residential builds.

Does Royal Renovators Inc. provide roofing for apartment complexes?

Yes. We provide roofing and flat roofing services for multi-unit residential buildings, and we work with co-op and condo boards on bids, budgets and scheduling.

What types of commercial buildings do you service?

Office buildings, warehouses, multi-family properties, retail spaces and mixed-use buildings. We provide complete commercial roof services, including full replacements on large properties.

Can commercial roofing projects be done without disrupting business?

Yes. Projects are scheduled to minimize disruption, and the property can usually stay occupied while the work is done.

Can commercial flat roofs handle rooftop equipment?

Yes. A properly installed flat roof is designed to support rooftop units. We work with you on where equipment should sit after the roof is replaced.

How often should commercial roofs be inspected?

At least once a year, with regular maintenance in between. Commercial roofs are larger and more expensive to replace, so catching problems early matters more. We also offer roof coatings that extend a roof’s life.

Siding, Gutters, Windows & Masonry

The rest of the building envelope, handled by the same crew.

Does Royal Renovators Inc. provide complete exterior renovations?

Yes. In addition to roofing, we provide siding, gutters, windows, masonry and other exterior services, and we frequently combine them in one renovation.

Does Royal Renovators Inc. provide siding services?

Yes. We install, repair and replace siding on residential and light commercial properties. Our vinyl and fiber cement siding improves insulation, curb appeal and protection from moisture. Vinyl is the material we usually recommend: economical, energy efficient and maintenance-free.

Can new siding reduce heating costs?

Yes. Properly installed siding improves insulation and energy efficiency, which lowers heating and cooling costs.

Do you install seamless gutters?

Yes. We install custom-fit seamless aluminum gutters that improve drainage and reduce the risk of leaks. The gutter-forming machines are on our trucks, so they are made on site.

Do gutters protect foundations?

Yes. Gutters keep water from pooling around the foundation and carry it safely away from the base of the house, where it would otherwise cause damage.

Do new windows improve energy efficiency?

Yes. Energy-efficient windows reduce heat loss in winter and heat gain in summer, which lowers utility bills. We install modern window systems designed for insulation, comfort and long-term performance.

Does Royal Renovators Inc. provide masonry services?

Yes. Brick repair, repointing, chimney restoration and facade structural repairs. Cracked mortar and deteriorating brick can weaken exterior walls, so masonry problems should not be left.

Weather & Emergencies

Storms, winter and what to do when the roof is leaking right now.

Do you offer emergency roof repair?

Yes. We provide emergency roof repair across Queens and NYC for leaks, storm damage and urgent structural concerns. We take emergency calls every day of the week, make the building safe first and quote the permanent repair afterwards.

Can storm damage be repaired quickly?

Yes. We repair wind, rain and storm-related damage, and same-day repairs are available when needed.

Do you inspect roofs after major storms?

Yes. A post-storm inspection finds hidden damage early, and our roof inspections are free.

Can NYC weather shorten a roof’s lifespan?

Yes. Heavy rain, snow, freeze-thaw cycles and high winds all wear on a roofing system, which is why a strong system matters so much in the city.

Can winter weather cause roof leaks?

Yes. Ice build-up and freeze-thaw cycles make materials expand and contract, which lets water in.

Can you work through the winter?

Yes, with limits. Some materials will not seal properly below a certain temperature, so we schedule around the forecast rather than the calendar and say plainly when a job should wait.

Do you help with insurance claims?

We document the damage with photographs and provide an itemized estimate your adjuster can work from. We are not public adjusters, so we do not negotiate the claim on your behalf.

Paperwork, older buildings and what is actually covered.

Do I need a permit to replace my roof?

Sometimes. It depends on the borough, the building and the scope of work. We check before quoting and discuss it with you, and we work with architects and DOB expeditors when a permit is required.

Is Royal Renovators Inc. experienced with NYC building codes?

Yes. With over 35 years in the industry, we understand NYC building regulations and compliance requirements, and we repair or replace roofs safely and compliantly.

Does Royal Renovators Inc. work on older NYC buildings?

Yes. We frequently work on brownstones, townhouses and aging flat-roof structures across Queens and NYC, many of them more than 100 years old.

What warranty do I get?

As GAF Master Elite contractors, on shingle roofs we can offer a 50-year factory-backed labor and material warranty. Flat roofs come with our 15-year warranty. Our own workmanship warranty is written into the contract.

Getting Started

What happens when you call, and what to have ready.

How do I schedule a roofing consultation?

Call (718) 414-6067 or send the form on our contact page. Live people answer the phone, and we can send an experienced estimator to your property the same day.

How quickly will I hear back?

Send the form and we get back to you within one business day. If the roof is actively leaking, call instead. The phone reaches someone seven days a week, including outside office hours.

Does an estimate cost anything?

No. We get up on the roof, photograph what we find and put a written, itemized price in front of you at no charge and with no obligation.

What should I tell you when I get in touch?
