Materials Built for Far Rockaway Flat Roofs

We install and repair EPDM, TPO, and modified bitumen with the drainage and waterproofing that stop ponding water and membrane damage.

EPDM RoofingA flexible rubber membrane for flat and low-slope roofs, with seams and penetrations fully sealed.
TPO RoofingA white single-ply membrane that reflects heat and keeps the roof cooler in summer sun.
Modified BitumenA layered asphalt-based system for low-slope roofs, with sealed transitions and drainage.
Drains, Seams & FlashingSlopes, drains, seams, and flashing points checked so heavy coastal rain runs off.

Do you provide roofing services in Far Rockaway, Queens?

Yes. Royal Roofing & Siding Far Rockaway provides residential and commercial roofing throughout Far Rockaway, including repairs, inspections, installations, and exterior improvements tailored to local properties.

Why do Far Rockaway homes need specialized roofing?

Strong winds, humidity, and salt air wear roofing materials faster, so durable installation and regular maintenance matter more here than inland.

How often should roofs be inspected near the coast?

At least once a year and after major storms, to catch early signs of damage and prevent costly structural issues.

Yes. We roof homes, apartment buildings, mixed-use properties, and commercial buildings, and adapt the solution to each building’s structure and use.
