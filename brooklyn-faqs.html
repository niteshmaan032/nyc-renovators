1. Roofing in Brooklyn

What types of roofing systems are common in Brooklyn?

Flat roofs are the most common, because so much of Brooklyn is brownstones, townhouses, and attached buildings. Shingle roofs are also used on pitched homes in some residential areas.

Do you work on Brooklyn brownstones?

Yes. We work on brownstones regularly and understand their structure, parapets, and drainage. Every repair or new roof is planned to protect both the building and its appearance.

Can you replace roofs on attached buildings?

Yes. Most of our Brooklyn work is on attached and semi-attached buildings. We seal the joints between neighboring roofs and keep disruption next door to a minimum.

Are your services suitable for older buildings?

Yes. Older Brooklyn properties are a large part of our work, and our approach focuses on durability while preserving the original building.

Do you handle commercial roofing?

Yes. We repair, replace, and maintain roofs on commercial properties across Brooklyn, with the system and schedule matched to the building and the business inside.

2. Flat Roofs

Do you offer flat roof repair in Brooklyn?

Yes. We trace leaks, patch damaged areas, and restore worn membranes, with repairs built to last rather than quick fixes. See our flat roofing services.

How long does a flat roof last in Brooklyn?

Usually 15 to 25 years, depending on the material and how well the roof is maintained. Regular upkeep can extend its life considerably.

What materials do you use for flat roofs?

EPDM, TPO, and modified bitumen. We choose the system based on durability, the building, and how the roof is used.

What are common roofing issues in Brooklyn properties?

Leaks, ponding water on flat roofs, and damaged membranes are the most common. On older buildings, aging drains and flashing often add to the problem.

3. Inspections & Repairs

How often should a roof be inspected in Brooklyn?

At least once a year, and again after any major storm. Regular inspections catch small problems before they become costly repairs.

How do I know if my roof needs replacement?

Frequent leaks, visible damage, and aging materials are the main signs. A professional inspection tells you whether a repair will do or a new roof makes more sense.

Do you handle emergency roof repairs?

Yes. We respond quickly to urgent roofing problems, because a fast repair keeps water from damaging the rest of the building.

Can roofing issues affect the building structure?

Yes. Leaks left untreated weaken framing, decking, and masonry over time, so it pays to deal with them early.

Do you offer maintenance services?

Yes. Routine maintenance extends the life of the roof, prevents surprise problems, and lowers long-term costs.

Do you inspect roofs before a property purchase?

Yes. A roof inspection before you buy shows potential problems early, so you can avoid unexpected repair costs after closing.

4. Gutters, Siding & Masonry

Do you install gutters in Brooklyn?

Yes. We install gutter systems sized for dense urban lots, so water drains away properly and does not damage the building.

What are the signs of gutter problems?

Overflowing water, sagging sections, and leaking joints. These should be fixed quickly before water reaches walls or the foundation.

Can poor drainage damage my property?

Yes. Poor drainage can lead to leaks and even foundation problems. A properly designed roof and gutter system is essential.

What siding options are available for Brooklyn homes?

We offer durable siding options for residential properties, chosen for weather protection and long-term performance.

Do you provide masonry services?

Yes. We repair brick, repoint mortar joints, and restore masonry to keep the structure sound and watertight.

What is repointing?

Repointing replaces worn mortar between bricks. It stops water from getting into the wall and strengthens the masonry.

5. Working With Us

Do you offer free roofing estimates?

Yes. We provide free estimates for residential and commercial property owners. We look at the roof and explain the options that fit the building.

How long does a roof installation take?

It depends on the size and condition of the property. Most projects are completed efficiently, with as little disruption as possible.

Do you work with landlords and property managers?

Yes. We regularly work with owners and managers of multi-unit buildings, and we schedule work to be efficient and reliable for tenants.

How do you ensure quality work?

Proven methods, quality materials, and experienced crews, with attention to the details that keep a roof watertight.

Which areas in Brooklyn do you serve?

All of Brooklyn, including Park Slope, Williamsburg, Bushwick, Bed-Stuy, Crown Heights, and the surrounding neighborhoods.
