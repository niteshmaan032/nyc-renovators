1. Roofing in the Bronx

The buildings we roof across the borough, from row houses to Hunts Point warehouses.

6 questions

What roofing systems are commonly used in the Bronx?

Flat roofing is the most common. Prewar apartment buildings, row houses, mixed-use buildings, and the warehouses in Hunts Point have low-slope roofs covered with EPDM, TPO, or modified bitumen. Detached homes in areas like Riverdale and Country Club often have pitched asphalt shingle roofs.

Do you provide roofing services for Bronx homes?

Yes. We repair, replace, and install flat and shingle roofs on homes across the Bronx, and we also handle siding, gutters, and masonry, so one team can protect the whole exterior.

Do you work on older Bronx buildings?

Yes. Prewar buildings are a large part of our work. We plan around their parapet walls, older masonry, internal drains, and old roof layers, so the new roof is sound and the building keeps its character.

Can you work on multifamily buildings?

Yes. We roof multifamily, co-op, and mixed-use buildings, and we schedule the work so tenants and storefronts can keep running with as little disruption as possible.

Do you work with commercial properties?

Yes. We repair and replace roofs on stores, offices, and the warehouses and industrial buildings around Hunts Point and Port Morris, including large flat membranes and the flashing around rooftop units.

What neighborhoods do you serve in the Bronx?

The whole borough, including Fordham, Riverdale, Pelham Bay, Throgs Neck, Morris Park, and Hunts Point. Our office is at 1231 Lafayette Ave, 2nd Floor, Bronx, NY 10474.

2. Flat Roofs & Materials

What goes on Bronx roofs, how long it lasts, and how to keep it working.

4 questions

What materials do you use for roofing?

On flat roofs we install EPDM, TPO, and modified bitumen. On pitched roofs we install architectural asphalt shingles over new underlayment. We choose the system based on the building, its drainage, and how the roof is used.

How long does a roof last in the Bronx?

It depends on the material, the quality of the installation, and the maintenance. On flat roofs, standing water and clogged drains shorten a roof’s life the most, so regular care adds years of service.

What are common roofing issues in the Bronx?

Leaks, ponding water, and aging membranes top the list. On row houses and prewar buildings we often find worn flashing at the parapet walls and blocked drains, and weather exposure speeds up the wear.

Do you provide maintenance services?

Yes. Flat roof maintenance means clearing drains, checking seams and flashing, and fixing small problems early, which extends the life of the roof. We recommend regular service.

3. Inspections & Repairs

When to call, what we check, and how we handle urgent leaks.

6 questions

How often should roofs be inspected in the Bronx?

Once a year, and again after a major storm or a hard winter. Regular inspections catch small problems before they turn into major repairs.

What are signs that a roof needs repair?

Leaks or water stains on the ceiling, visible damage to the roof surface, sagging areas, and water that stays on a flat roof long after rain. Acting early prevents further damage.

Do you offer roof repair services in the Bronx?

Yes. We repair leaks, damaged membranes and shingles, failed flashing, and structural problems, with repairs built for long-term reliability rather than quick patches.

Do you provide emergency roofing services?

Yes. We respond quickly to urgent leaks and storm damage to protect the building from further harm. Call 718-395-5061, seven days a week from 7:00 am to 11:00 pm.

Do you provide roof inspections before repairs?

Yes. Every repair starts with an inspection, so we find the cause of the problem and recommend the right fix instead of guessing.

Can roofing problems affect property value?

Yes. A roof in poor condition lowers a property’s value and can complicate a sale. Timely repairs and maintenance help preserve it.

4. Gutters & Masonry

Keeping water off the walls and away from the foundation.

6 questions

Do you install gutter systems in the Bronx?

Yes. We install and repair gutter systems sized to carry water away from the building, which prevents water damage to walls and foundations.

What are common gutter issues?

Clogs, leaking joints, and gutters pulling out of alignment. Each one affects drainage and can send water into the walls.

Can poor drainage damage buildings?

Yes. Poor drainage leads to leaks, damaged walls, and even foundation problems. Working drains, scuppers, and gutters are essential, especially on flat roofs.

Do you offer masonry services in the Bronx?

Yes. We repair and restore brick, including repointing and parapet wall repair, to keep the building’s structure sound and watertight.

What causes masonry damage?

Moisture, freeze-and-thaw cycles, and aging mortar. Parapet walls on prewar buildings and row houses take the most weather, so regular inspection matters.

Can masonry issues lead to structural problems?

Yes. Untreated cracks and loose brick let water in and weaken the wall over time, so timely repairs are essential.
