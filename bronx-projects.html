Our process

How We Run Every Project

The same four steps on every Bronx job, from a row house leak to a full multifamily roof.

  1. 01

    Assess the roof

    We check the roof’s age, structure, and drainage, plus every seam, flashing, and parapet.

  2. 02

    Choose the system

    You get a written scope and the roof system that suits the building and its upkeep.

  3. 03

    Our crew installs

    Our own crew works with premium materials and leaves the roof watertight each day.

  4. 04

    Final walkthrough

    We walk the finished roof with you, test the drains, and clean up the site.
