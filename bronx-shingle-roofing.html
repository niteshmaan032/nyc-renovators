Warning signs

When to Repair or Replace Your Shingle Roof

Rain, winter freeze cycles, wind, and summer heat wear shingles down over time. Catch these early and a repair is often enough.

  • Curling or cupping edgesShingle edges lifting or bowing as they age
  • Cracked or broken tabsSplits from freeze-thaw cycles and heat
  • Missing shinglesBare patches after high winds or a storm
  • Granule lossGrit collecting in gutters and downspouts
  • Leaks or attic stainsDamp decking or ceiling stains after rain
  • Loose flashingGaps at chimneys, walls, vents, and valleys

Repair or Replace?

A repair often makes sense when

  • The damage is in one area
  • The rest of the roof is in good shape
  • The decking underneath is solid

A replacement is usually better when

  • Shingles are worn across the roof
  • Leaks keep coming back after repairs
  • The decking is soft or water-damaged

Shingle Roofing FAQs

See All FAQs Get a Free Estimate
Do you provide shingle roofing services throughout the Bronx?

Yes. We install, repair, replace, and inspect shingle roofs across the Bronx, including Riverdale, Country Club, Throgs Neck, and Pelham Bay.

How do I know if my shingle roof needs repair?

Look for missing shingles, leaks, curling edges, cracked shingles, and granules collecting in the gutters. If you see any of these, call us for an inspection.

How long does a shingle roof last?

It depends on the material and how well the roof is maintained, but many shingle roofs last 20 years or more. Good ventilation and flashing help a roof reach that.

Can damaged shingles be repaired without replacing the whole roof?

Yes. If the damage is localized and the rest of the roof is in good condition, a repair may be all you need.

Can a new shingle roof improve home value?

Yes. A professionally installed roof improves curb appeal and overall property value, and it gives buyers confidence if you sell.

Do you offer inspections and estimates?

Yes. We provide professional inspections and free estimates based on the roof’s condition and your project. Call 718-395-5061 to schedule yours.
