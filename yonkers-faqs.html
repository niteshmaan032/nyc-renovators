1. Weather & Seasons

Snow, ice dams, spring rain, and storms: how Yonkers weather wears on a roof.

8 questions

How does Yonkers’ winter weather affect asphalt shingle roofs?

Snow accumulation, ice dams, and repeated freezing and thawing can shorten the lifespan of asphalt shingles, especially when the roof has poor ventilation or damaged flashing.

Can ice dams damage my roof during Yonkers winters?

Yes. Ice dams can force melting snow beneath shingles, causing water damage to the roof decking, insulation, ceilings, and interior walls.

Does heavy snowfall affect roof flashing?

Repeated freezing and thawing can loosen flashing around chimneys, skylights, vents, and roof transitions, which makes water intrusion more likely.

What causes roof leaks during spring in Yonkers?

Melting snow, clogged gutters, damaged flashing, and worn roofing materials often lead to leaks once the spring rain begins.

Should I have my roof inspected after a severe storm in Yonkers?

Yes. High winds and heavy rain can loosen shingles, damage flashing, and create hidden leaks that are not visible from the ground.

How can I prepare my roof for winter in Yonkers?

Schedule a roof inspection, clean the gutters, trim overhanging branches, check the flashing, and fix any damaged shingles before the cold weather arrives.

Do homes near the Hudson River experience different roofing conditions?

Properties closer to the river can see stronger winds and higher moisture levels, which makes regular roof maintenance especially important.

What time of year is best for replacing a roof in Yonkers?

Spring, summer, and fall generally offer the best weather, but roof replacements can be completed year-round depending on conditions and how urgent the work is.

2. Older Homes & Materials

Colonial, Tudor, and Cape Cod homes, roof layers, ventilation, and trees.

6 questions

What roofing problems are most common for homes in Yonkers?

Heavy snowfall, freeze-thaw cycles, aging shingles, clogged gutters, and wind-driven rain cause most of the roof damage we see. Regular inspections catch these issues before they become costly repairs.

Do older homes in neighborhoods like Crestwood and Park Hill require different roofing solutions?

Yes. Many older homes there have aging roof structures and ventilation systems that need a careful evaluation before repairs or replacement, so the new work performs for the long term.

What roofing material is best for Colonial homes in Yonkers?

Architectural asphalt shingles are often an excellent choice. They are durable, weather resistant, and have a traditional look that suits Colonial-style homes.

Do older roofs in Yonkers often have multiple roofing layers?

Yes. Many older properties have been re-roofed over existing materials. We inspect the roof structure to decide whether a complete tear-off is recommended before installing a new system.

Can poor attic ventilation shorten the lifespan of my roof?

Yes. Inadequate ventilation traps heat and moisture, which speeds up shingle deterioration and makes ice dams more likely in winter.

Can mature trees around my Yonkers property damage my roof?

Yes. Falling branches, leaves, and debris can damage roofing materials, clog gutters, and trap moisture, raising the risk of leaks and early roof wear.

3. Inspections & Repairs

When to inspect, signs of hidden damage, and repair or replace.

6 questions

How often should homeowners in Yonkers schedule a roof inspection?

We recommend a professional roof inspection at least once a year, and again after major storms or heavy snowfall.

Are roof inspections important before buying a home in Yonkers?

Absolutely. A professional roof inspection can find hidden issues that a standard home inspection may miss, so you can make an informed decision.

What signs indicate my roof may have hidden storm damage?

Missing shingles, lifted flashing, ceiling stains, damp attic insulation, granules in the gutters, and visible debris impact are all common signs.

Can roof repairs increase the lifespan of my existing roof?

If damage is addressed early, professional repairs can significantly extend the life of your roof and delay the need for replacement.

Do you help homeowners decide whether repairs or replacement are more appropriate?

Yes. We perform a detailed inspection and recommend the option that offers the best long-term value for your roof’s overall condition.

How long does a typical roof replacement take in Yonkers?

Most residential roof replacements are completed within one to three days, depending on roof size, weather, and project complexity.

4. Flat & Commercial Roofs

Flat roofs on apartment, commercial, church, and school buildings.

5 questions

Why do flat roofs on Yonkers commercial buildings require regular inspections?

Flat roofs are more prone to ponding water, drainage problems, and membrane wear. Annual inspections find problems early and extend the roof’s service life.

Are commercial flat roofs in Yonkers different from residential flat roofs?

Yes. Commercial roofs often cover larger areas with mechanical equipment, drainage systems, and specialized membranes that need different installation methods.

What roofing systems do you recommend for commercial properties in Yonkers?

It depends on the building’s design and use, but EPDM, TPO, and Modified Bitumen are popular choices for commercial properties throughout Yonkers. Compare the three systems.

Do you install roofing systems for churches and schools in Yonkers?

Yes. We roof churches, schools, municipal buildings, office buildings, retail properties, and other commercial facilities.

Do you repair roofs on multifamily homes in Yonkers?

Yes. We work on single-family homes, duplexes, apartment buildings, condominiums, and other multifamily residential properties.

5. Gutters & Working With Us

Gutters, the neighborhoods we serve, and why owners choose us.

5 questions

Why do gutters play such an important role in protecting Yonkers homes?

Working gutters carry water away from the roof and foundation, which helps prevent leaks, erosion, siding damage, and basement moisture.

Can clogged gutters cause roof leaks?

Yes. Overflowing gutters let water back up beneath roofing materials, raising the risk of fascia damage, leaks, and structural deterioration.

Which neighborhoods do you serve in Yonkers?

Getty Square, Crestwood, Bryn Mawr, Park Hill, Colonial Heights, Homefield, Ludlow, Lincoln Park, Nepperhan, Northwest Yonkers, and the surrounding communities.

Why should I choose a local Yonkers roofing contractor instead of an out-of-town company?

A local contractor understands Yonkers’ climate, neighborhoods, building styles, and seasonal roofing challenges, and can recommend solutions suited to the area.

Why do homeowners throughout Yonkers continue choosing Royal Roofing Yonkers?

Our 35+ years of experience, commitment to quality workmanship, transparent communication, premium materials, and local knowledge of Yonkers homes, neighborhoods, and weather. Our goal is a roof that delivers long-term protection and peace of mind.
