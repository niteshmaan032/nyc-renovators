Contact & Estimate FAQs

Response times, free estimates, and the areas we cover.

Call (914) 228-9588 See All FAQs
How quickly will I hear back?

Send the form and we’ll call you back within the hour. You can also call (914) 228-9588 any day of the week between 7:00 am and 11:00 pm.

Does an estimate cost anything?

No. We offer free roof inspections and detailed estimates, so you understand the condition of your roof and can decide without pressure.

What should I tell you when I get in touch?

The property address, what is happening with the roof, and when we can come by to inspect it.

Which areas do you cover?

All of Yonkers, including Getty Square, Crestwood, Park Hill, Bryn Mawr, Lincoln Park, Colonial Heights, Homefield, Ludlow, Nepperhan, and Northwest Yonkers, plus nearby Westchester communities.

Do you work on commercial properties?

Yes. We roof offices, retail centers, apartment complexes, schools, churches, warehouses, and mixed-use buildings, as well as single-family homes.

Royal Roofing Yonkers provides licensed residential and commercial roofing services throughout Yonkers and nearby Westchester communities. From flat roof systems and shingle roof repairs to complete replacements, we deliver dependable workmanship, quality materials, and honest service.
