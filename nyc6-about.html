About Royal Renovators Inc.

Top-Rated Queens Roofing Contractor With 35+ Years of Experience

Royal Renovators Inc. is a trusted Queens-based roofing contractor serving New York City since 1988. As a family-owned and operated, BBB A+ rated company, we specialize in flat roofing, shingle systems, siding, gutters, masonry, and complete exterior renovations for residential and commercial buildings.

From brownstones in Brooklyn to high-rise properties in Manhattan and multi-family buildings in the Bronx, our approach combines 35+ years of large-scale experience with local execution, so homeowners, property managers, and building owners work with crews that understand both the technical demands of roofing and their neighborhood’s buildings.

Experience & results

A Local Roofing Company Built on Experience & Results

Royal Renovators Inc. started as a small, locally focused roofing company and has grown into one of the most reliable names in roofing throughout Queens. With over three decades in the industry, we understand the challenges that come with New York City buildings: aging structures, strict codes, tight access, and roofs that take a beating from the weather.
