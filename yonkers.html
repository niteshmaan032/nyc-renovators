Do homes in Yonkers need roof inspections after heavy winter snow?

Yes. Snow and repeated freeze-thaw cycles can loosen shingles, damage flashing, and create hidden leaks. An inspection after winter finds small problems before they become costly repairs.

Which roofing material works best for older homes in Yonkers?

Many older Colonial, Tudor, and Cape Cod homes benefit from architectural asphalt shingles, which are durable and suit the home’s original design. The best choice depends on the roof’s structure and your long-term goals.

Why are roof leaks common during the spring in Yonkers?

As snow melts and spring rain arrives, water finds its way through damaged flashing, worn shingles, or clogged drainage. Seasonal inspections catch these issues before they reach your interior.

Do you install roofing systems for commercial properties in Yonkers?

Yes. We provide flat roofing, roof replacement, repairs, preventive maintenance, and waterproofing for offices, retail centers, apartment complexes, schools, churches, warehouses, and other commercial properties.

What signs indicate my roof may need immediate attention?

Water stains on ceilings, missing shingles, granules in gutters, sagging areas, loose flashing, mold growth, and recurring leaks all mean the roof should be inspected as soon as possible.
