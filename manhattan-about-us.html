Our approach

How We Approach Every Manhattan Roof

Every project starts with a detailed evaluation of the property, not a price over the phone.

Rain, wind, and the structural stress of dense city blocks wear down roofs, drains, and masonry over time. We check how those forces are affecting your building, then recommend the work that will last.

Book a Free Inspection

  1. Inspect the whole roof

    Roof condition, drainage, material wear, and structure, checked first.

  2. Recommend what fits

    Practical options based on the building’s condition, not quick patches.

  3. Use durable materials

    TPO, EPDM, SBS, and shingles chosen for city weather and exposure.

  4. Finish clean & on time

    Organized crews, clear updates, and little disruption for residents.
