1. Roofing in Manhattan

Building types, roof systems, and the neighborhoods we serve.

6 questionsContents

What roofing systems are common on Manhattan buildings?

Flat (low-slope) roofs are the most common, because so much of Manhattan is high-rise, co-op, condo, and commercial buildings. We install and repair TPO, EPDM, and modified bitumen (SBS) systems built for durability and good drainage, plus shingles where a building has a pitched roof.

Do you work on high-rise buildings?

Yes. We work on high-rise and commercial roofs, including commercial flat roofing projects. Each job is planned around the structure, roof access, and safety requirements of the building.

Do you work on brownstones and townhouses?

Yes. Most brownstones and townhouses have a low-slope roof behind a parapet or cornice, reached by a roof hatch or bulkhead. We repair and replace these roofs and the flashing at parapets, chimneys, and skylights. In a historic district, visible exterior work may also need Landmarks Preservation Commission approval, so we factor that into the plan.

Do you work on older and prewar buildings?

Yes. We work on prewar co-ops and historic buildings as well as modern construction. Older roofs often hide several layers of past work, original parapets, and masonry that need care, so we tailor the approach to the building.

Do you work on commercial and mixed-use properties?

Yes. We roof commercial and mixed-use buildings, including storefronts with apartments above. Each project is planned around the building’s requirements and the businesses and tenants inside.

Which Manhattan neighborhoods do you serve?

We work across Manhattan, including Midtown, the Upper East Side, the Upper West Side, Harlem, Chelsea, the West Village, and the neighborhoods in between.

2. Co-ops, Condos & Building Access

Boards, managing agents, high-rise access, terraces, and bulkheads.

6 questionsContents

Do you work with co-op and condo boards?

Yes. We put the scope, materials, schedule, and cost in writing so the board and managing agent can review and approve the work, and we answer the board’s questions before anything starts.

Do you coordinate with building management and the super?

Yes. We work with managing agents and supers on roof access, work hours, elevator use, resident notices, and approvals, so the project runs smoothly for everyone in the building.

How do crews and materials get onto a high-rise roof?

We plan access with building management before work starts: how crews reach the roof (freight elevator, stairs, or roof hatch), how materials and debris move without damaging lobbies and hallways, and which hours work is allowed. Every building is different, so access is part of the estimate.

Can you fix a leak under a rooftop terrace or deck?

Yes. On many Manhattan roofs the membrane sits under pavers, decking, or planters. Leaks usually start at drains, flashing, or seams below the surface, so part of the finish may need to come up to find and fix the source. We tell you what has to be lifted before work begins.

Do you repair bulkheads and other rooftop structures?

Yes. Stair and elevator bulkheads, parapets, and equipment curbs are common leak points where walls meet the roof. We repair the flashing, coping, and membrane around them, and the masonry when the walls need it.

Will the roof work disrupt residents?

We plan for minimal disruption to residents and tenants: agreed work hours, protected common areas, and a tidy roof at the end of each day. Noisy work such as a tear-off is scheduled with management in advance.

3. Inspections & Repairs

When to inspect, what to watch for, and what to do about a leak.

6 questionsContents

How often should a Manhattan roof be inspected?

Once a year, plus after severe weather such as heavy storms, high winds, or a hard freeze. Regular inspections catch small problems early and keep the building safe and protected.

What are the signs a roof needs repair?

Leaks, ceiling stains on the top floor, cracks or blisters in the membrane, water standing on the roof after rain, and loose flashing are the common signs. An inspection shows whether a repair is enough.

What are the most common roofing problems in Manhattan?

Leaks, drainage problems, and membrane damage. City conditions add wear: foot traffic around rooftop equipment, pollution, and freeze-thaw cycles all shorten a roof’s life.

Do you inspect the roof before recommending repairs?

Yes. We inspect first so the roof repair fixes the actual cause of the problem, not just the symptom. You get clear options based on what we find.

Do you handle emergency roof repairs?

Yes. Call 212-457-1331 about an active leak and we’ll talk through the emergency repair and the next step. Acting quickly limits damage to the apartments below. Our phone hours are 7:00 am to 11:00 pm, seven days a week.

Do you offer roof maintenance?

Yes. Routine maintenance, such as clearing drains, resealing flashing, and fixing small wear, extends the life of flat and shingle roofs and reduces long-term repair costs.

4. Gutters, Drainage & Masonry

Keeping water off the facade and out of the walls.

6 questionsContents

Do you install gutters and drainage on Manhattan buildings?

Yes. We install and maintain gutters, leaders, and roof drainage designed for city buildings. Taller buildings collect more water, so their drainage has to be sized and routed for it.

Can poor drainage damage a building?

Yes. Water that pools on the roof or spills over the edge damages the facade, masonry, and structure over time. Clogged gutters and drains also back water up under the roof and cause leaks.

Do you provide masonry services?

Yes. We offer masonry repair, repointing, and restoration for brick and stone, including parapets and chimneys, to keep the structure sound and watertight.

What causes masonry damage in Manhattan?

Weather exposure, freeze-thaw cycles, pollution, and water getting into mortar joints all weaken masonry over time. Regular maintenance and timely repointing prevent deterioration.

What is facade restoration?

The repair and upkeep of a building’s exterior walls, including masonry, joints, and trim. It protects the structure and keeps the building looking its best.

Can masonry problems affect safety?

Yes. Loose or cracked masonry on parapets and facades can put people below and the structure at risk, so timely repairs are essential.

5. Working With Us

Estimates, timelines, materials, and the standard we work to.

6 questionsContents

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Call 212-457-1331 to set up an inspection and a free estimate tailored to the building.

How long does a roofing project take?

It depends on the roof’s size and complexity and, in co-ops and condos, on board approval and building access. You get a timeline with the estimate, and we work efficiently without cutting corners.

What roofing materials do you use?

Durable materials suited to city conditions: TPO, EPDM, and modified bitumen (SBS) on flat roofs, and shingles on pitched roofs. The right choice depends on the building, how the roof is used, and the budget.

How do you ensure quality work?

Experienced crews, proven installation methods, and close attention to the flashing, seams, and drains where most roofs fail. Every project is built to NYC’s building codes.

Do you offer long-term solutions?

Yes. We focus on durability and performance, so the roof needs less upkeep and protects the building’s value and safety for years.

Why choose Royal Roofing & Siding NYC?

We focus on Manhattan buildings, from brownstones to prewar co-ops and high-rises, and we plan around boards, managers, and tight access. Our priorities are efficiency, safety, and long-term performance.
