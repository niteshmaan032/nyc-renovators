Our process

How We Run Every Project

The same four steps on every Far Rockaway job, from a storm leak to a full roof replacement.

  1. 01

    Inspect the roof

    We check the surface, seams, flashing, drains, and slopes to find the real cause of the problem.

  2. 02

    Explain the options

    You get honest recommendations and transparent pricing, with no hidden costs, before any work starts.

  3. 03

    Install for the coast

    Durable materials chosen for salt air and wind, and the roof left watertight at the end of each day.

  4. 04

    Final walkthrough

    We walk the finished roof with you, check the drainage, and clean up the site completely.
